Roxy Browser Review: Strengths, Trade-offs, and Who It’s For

A reference guide based on MR AIT’s Roxy Browser video course

Roxy Browser keeps coming up in 2026 “best antidetect browser” conversations — it’s MR AIT’s top pick this year. This reference guide condenses what the course actually shows about the product, so you can decide if it fits your workflow before installing anything.

What Roxy Browser is

A Chromium-based, multi-profile anti-detect browser. Every profile is a complete separate identity: its own cookies, its own fingerprint (160+ adjustable parameters), and its own proxy IP. The stack is provisioned for you — launch a profile and the isolated browser opens in seconds.

Where it shines

  • A dashboard you don’t fight — clean and lightweight; the course stresses how quickly beginners find everything.
  • Project-based organization — group profiles per client or campaign; the demonstrated workflow runs 100+ accounts without chaos.
  • Proxy flexibility — attach, test, and swap proxies per profile with a few clicks.
  • Built-in extras — synchronizer for parallel actions, team collaboration, extensions management, and an API for automation.
  • Learning curve — backed by a full free video course (this site is its written version).

The trade-offs to know

  • Proxies are on you. The browser isolates identities, but a profile without a quality proxy isn’t really isolated. Budget for proxies — it’s the difference between the tool working and not.
  • The free tier has limits. Reviews report a free plan with a small number of profiles and a trial of the full feature set; serious multi-account work means a paid plan. Start with the free trial and free plan to see the workflow before paying.
  • Discipline still matters. As the course says: proper setup, quality proxies, smart scaling, consistent execution. The tool removes technical friction, not the need for a process.

Who gets the most out of it

Social media managers and agencies running client accounts, affiliate marketers, e-commerce sellers with multiple storefronts, automation users who want the API — and freelancers selling profile setup and account management as a service (the first video’s exact pitch).

How it compares

The course positions Roxy against the established names — Multilogin, GoLogin, Dolphin Anty, Logii. The differentiators it demonstrates: dashboard speed and cleanliness, fast profile creation, and project-based organization at 100+ account scale. Competitor “vs” pages exist on all sides, so test with a free plan or trial and compare the workflow yourself.
