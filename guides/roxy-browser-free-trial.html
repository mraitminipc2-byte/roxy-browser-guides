Roxy Browser Free Trial and Free Plan: How to Start Without Paying

A reference guide based on MR AIT’s Roxy Browser video course

Before you commit to any antidetect browser, test it with a real profile. Here’s the no-cost path shown in the course.

1. Download and install

Get the app via the course’s link: free Roxy Browser download. Installation takes a couple of minutes and lands you on the dashboard — the same walkthrough shown in Lesson 2.

2. What the free tier includes

Third-party reviews report a free plan covering a handful of active profiles (about five), enough to create profiles, attach proxies, and feel the fingerprint workflow. A trial of the full feature set is also offered — the course’s Discord community (join here) helps with trial activation, which is where trial codes and support live.

3. What to test first

  1. Create two profiles and launch them side by side — confirm cookies and sessions don’t leak between them.
  2. Attach a test proxy to one profile and check its reported IP and country.
  3. Group both profiles into a Project and feel the organization model.
  4. Open a public fingerprint test page in each profile and compare what sites see.
Then follow the course: Step 1 gets you free proxies, and the full walkthrough tours every feature — synchronizer, teams, and API included.

Do you need a card for the trial?

The free plan path needs no payment details. Paid trials or plans, when you upgrade, follow the billing flow in the app. Current limits and pricing live on the official site — they change, so verify there before planning a large deployment.
