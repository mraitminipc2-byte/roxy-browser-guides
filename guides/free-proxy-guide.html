How to Get a Free Proxy (Course Step 1)

Based on the video "Antidetect Browser Course: How to Get Free Proxy (Step 1)" by MR AIT · watch on YouTube

Episode 1 of the antidetect browser course starts where every good setup starts: proxies. Before you create profiles or touch fingerprints, you need to understand what a proxy does and how to get one you can actually rely on.

What is a proxy?

A proxy is a middleman server between you and the websites you visit: your traffic goes out through the proxy's IP address instead of your own. In a profile-based workflow this matters because IP address is one of the loudest signals a site sees. If five profiles all connect from your home IP, they look related no matter how good their fingerprints are — one flag can cascade into bans and shadow bans across all of them. One clean IP per profile keeps each identity isolated.

What the video covers

  • What a proxy is and the role it plays in profile-based workflows.
  • How to get started with a free proxy trial so you can test right away.
  • How to export free proxies and bring them into your antidetect browser.

Test before you commit

A free trial is the right way to start because it lets you evaluate three things before spending money:

  • Reliability — does the connection stay up, or drop mid-session?
  • Geo-targeting — can you pick the country your profile appears from?
  • Speed — is the proxy fast enough for real work, not just a ping test?

Quick start checklist

  1. Start a free proxy trial (link from the video).
  2. Export the proxies in the format your browser expects (host, port, user, password).
  3. Add one proxy to a test profile in Roxy Browser.
  4. Verify connectivity: check the IP and country the profile now shows.
  5. Only then start attaching proxies to your real profiles.
